Frequently Asked
Questions

How does yacht share ownership work?

Yacht share ownership allows you to enjoy the benefits of owning a luxury yacht without the full financial and operational burden. Instead of purchasing an entire vessel, you acquire a defined share, which gives you guaranteed access to the yacht for a set number of days or weeks each year.

Neptune Oceanic structures each ownership group carefully, ensuring a limited number of owners per yacht. This means availability is reliable, scheduling is fair, and the yacht is never overused. Usage is managed through a transparent booking system that balances peak and off peak periods across all owners.

How is booking time managed between owners?

Booking time is managed through our proprietary online scheduling platform. Each owner receives a fair allocation of peak and off-peak periods across the year, ensuring no single owner dominates prime dates. Advance booking windows, waitlists, and swap options are all available through your owner portal.

What costs are included in ownership?

Your annual ownership fee covers maintenance, insurance, berthing, cleaning, and routine servicing. We handle all the operational complexity so you simply arrive and enjoy. Any extraordinary repairs are shared equitably among the ownership group and communicated transparently.

Where are the yachts located?

Neptune Oceanic yachts are currently based in the Whitsundays, Queensland — one of Australia's premier sailing destinations. We are expanding to additional locations and marina options. Contact us for the latest availability by region.

Do I need boating experience to join?

No prior boating experience is required. We offer fully crewed options for those who prefer a hands-off experience, as well as bareboat access for licensed skippers. Our team can also connect you with accredited training courses if you wish to develop your skills as part of the ownership experience.

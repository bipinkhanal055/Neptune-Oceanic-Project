Life Aboard a Riviera 6000 Sport Yacht Platinum

After years of chartering, James and Eleanor chose the Riviera 6000 Sport Yacht Platinum for its balance of performance, comfort and intelligent design. Built by Riviera, it offered the space they needed for family cruising, without feeling overwhelming to manage as a couple.

Watch Testimonial

First Impressions

From their first coastal passage, the yacht delivered a smooth, confident ride. Clear helm visibility, intuitive controls and a stable hull gave them immediate reassurance in varied conditions.

At anchor, the cockpit and saloon transformed into welcoming spaces for relaxed evenings with friends and family.

Life on Board

The open layout made entertaining effortless, while the accommodation provided privacy and comfort for longer stays. Morning coffee on deck, spontaneous weekend escapes and extended summer cruising quickly became part of their routine.

What began as a purchase soon became a lifestyle shift.

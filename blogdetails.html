The Modern Sport Yacht Advantage

February 24, 2025

The 2024 edition of the Riviera 6000 Sport Yacht Platinum represents a refined balance of performance, luxury and intelligent design. Created by the renowned Australian builder Riviera, this model continues the brand's reputation for craftsmanship, seaworthiness and owner focused innovation.

Designed for Effortless Cruising

From the moment you step aboard, the 6000 Sport Yacht Platinum delivers a seamless indoor and outdoor experience. Wide walkways, a generous cockpit and an inviting swim platform make entertaining simple, while the saloon flows naturally into the galley and helm. Large windows invite natural light throughout, enhancing the sense of space and connection to the sea.

The open plan layout has been carefully considered to maximise comfort during extended cruising. Whether hosting friends for the weekend or embarking on a longer coastal passage, every element supports relaxed and practical living on board.

Performance With Confidence

Built on Riviera's proven hull design, the 6000 Sport Yacht Platinum offers confident handling and efficient cruising capability. Advanced engineering and modern propulsion systems provide responsive performance, while maintaining stability and comfort in varied sea conditions.

The helm station is equipped with contemporary navigation technology, clear sightlines and ergonomic controls, ensuring the skipper remains in complete command. The result is a yacht that feels equally at home in open waters or when manoeuvring into a busy marina.

Refined Accommodation

Below deck, the accommodation layout is designed to offer privacy and comfort. Spacious cabins, quality finishes and thoughtful storage solutions create an environment suited to both owners and guests. Attention to detail in materials and lighting reinforces the sense of understated luxury throughout.

The master suite provides a private retreat, while guest cabins are equally welcoming. Each space reflects Riviera's commitment to premium craftsmanship and long term durability.
